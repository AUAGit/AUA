ABOUT THE CAR(S)

E46 323Ci

The first of many. The BMW 3 Series (E46) is the fourth generation of the BMW 3 Series range of compact executive cars manufactured by German automaker BMW. The 323Ci was one of the first variants of the E46 BMW 3 Series to be introduced. Debuting for the 2000 model year, the 323Ci was available as a two door coupe or two door convertible. Production of the 323Ci would only last one year before it was replaced by the E46 BMW 325Ci. Powered by a 2.5L M52TÜB25 inline-6, our car makes a cool 168 HP and 181 lb-ft of torque. Sitting on some Apex Wheels, MaXpeeding rods.
